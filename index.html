About us

Founded in July 2025, our agricultural and groundworks business is run by brothers Harry and James Peters, combining a shared passion for farming with a commitment to hard work, reliability and quality workmanship.

Born into farming families, with both sides of the family having strong roots in dairy farming, agriculture has always been a part of our lives. Growing up surrounded by farming has given us a genuine understanding of the industry, the land and the demands of agricultural work.

Now operating from our family farm in Silverstone, we are proud to build on those family roots while providing a professional and reliable service to our customers.

Whether it’s agricultural contracting or groundworks, we take pride in every job we undertake, with a practical, hands-on approach and a determination to deliver work to a high standard.

As a family-run brother duo, we believe in honest communication, attention to detail and building lasting relationships with our customers.
