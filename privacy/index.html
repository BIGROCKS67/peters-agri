Information we collect and why

When using the enquiry form, we receive the details supplied by yourselves. We use these details to reply about the enquiry, quote and book the works in. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before a contract.

If you message us on WhatsApp or ring us, we will see your number and information shared, on our own phones.

Who else handles your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes the form to our email inbox.
  • Microsoft Outlook holds our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries any messages you choose to send us there.

Some of these companies are based in the US, so your details may be stored outside the UK.

How long we keep your information for

Enquiries that do not progress are deleted after 12 months. Details for works completed and in progress are kept for 6 years for our tax records.

You can also raise a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
