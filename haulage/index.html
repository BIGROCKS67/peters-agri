What we can cover

  1. 01Straw and hay haulageTransportation of straw, hay and other agricultural bales.
  2. 02Machinery haulageMoving diggers, tractors, farm machinery and other heavy equipment.
  3. 03Agricultural haulageTransporting agricultural equipment, supplies and materials between farms and sites.
  4. 04Material haulageMoving materials such as stone, aggregates, sand, gravel and other construction supplies.
  5. 05Compost and woodchip haulageMoving compost and woodchip between locations.
  6. 06General tractor haulageFlexible haulage solutions for a variety of loads and transportation requirements.

Good to know

What can you move?

Straw, hay and other bales, diggers, tractors and farm machinery on the low loader, and stone, aggregates, sand, gravel, compost and woodchip.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Northamptonshire, Buckinghamshire, Warwickshire, Oxfordshire and Leicestershire from the farm in Silverstone, mostly around Towcester, Brackley and Buckingham.

How do I get a price?

Call Harry on 07712 229940 or James on 07496 962495, or send a WhatsApp with a photo and a few details of the job. We work 7am to 7pm.
