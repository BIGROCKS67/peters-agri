Digger Hire with Driver: What's Included

When you hire our digger, one of us comes with it and drives it. Our JCB Fastrac and dump trailer can cart the spoil away. Here's what's included, the jobs it suits and what to sort before we arrive.

What you get

  • the digger, with one of us driving it on every job
  • our JCB Fastrac and dump trailer to cart spoil away, so it doesn't sit in a heap on site
  • farm, domestic and commercial jobs

Jobs it suits

  • digging out and levelling for hardstanding, tracks and bases
  • footings and trenches
  • ditching, and clearing blocked ditches
  • drainage and field drains
  • site clearance and preparation, including roots and scrub
  • tree shearing with an excavator attachment

Before we arrive

  • Check the way in. Tell us about narrow gates, soft ground or low wires.
  • Find out where underground pipes and cables run. Linesearch before u dig (lsbud.co.uk) is a free UK service that checks for many of them.
  • Decide where the spoil goes, carted away or kept on site.
  • Send us photos of the job and where you are on WhatsApp.

Getting a price

Every job is different, so we price from photos and a few details. Call Harry on 07712 229940 or James on 07496 962495, or send a WhatsApp.

Questions

Does the digger come with a driver?

Yes. One of us drives the digger on every job.

Can you take the spoil away?

Yes. We load it into the dump trailer behind our JCB Fastrac and cart it away.

Do you work on houses and gardens?

Yes. We take on farm, domestic and commercial jobs.
