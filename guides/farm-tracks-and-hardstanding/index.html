Farm Tracks and Hardstanding: How We Build Them

We build stone farm tracks and hardstanding with our digger, Fastrac and dump trailer. Here's how we do it, step by step, with photos from a real job.

How we build it

  • Strip the ground back. We dig out the topsoil and soft ground and cart it away.
  • Lay membrane. A ground membrane goes down to keep the stone and the soil underneath apart.
  • Stone it up. We bring in stone and spread it.
  • Level it. The stone is levelled and firmed into a solid surface.

Keep the water away

Water is what ruins tracks. A clear ditch alongside helps the water run off instead of sitting on the track. We clear and re-dig ditches as well, so it can be done on the same job.

Jobs we've done

New hardstanding beside a field, a stone track along a field edge, and a track along the floor of an old railway cutting after taking down the bridge. You can see them on our digger services page.

Getting a price

Send us photos of where the track or hardstanding is going on WhatsApp, with rough sizes, and we'll come back with a price.

Questions

Why lay membrane under the stone?

It keeps the stone and the soil underneath apart, so the stone doesn't get pushed into soft ground.

Do you take the old soil away?

Yes. We load it into the dump trailer behind our Fastrac and cart it away.
